# v1.1 — release notes

**Public Release v1.1**, dated 2026-11-04. *Sapiens Novus — Constitutio Intermundia*.

## Summary

v1.1 is the public release. It is a hardening and clarity pass on the v1.02 constitution. The twelve axioms are word for word the v1.02 axioms. Around them, every chapter has been rewritten into clearer sentences. Numbers, VCI weights, the Ne floor, formulas, room names, and the `Status: **LOCKED**` lines are unchanged. The earlier authorized fixes remain: the glossary escape for `E_net`, three Pactum lines that were editor's notes and are now rules, the Prioritas parenthetical turned into a sentence, and blank lines so the IX.1 status line and the chapter headings render. IX.2 sits before IX.3. Nothing was renumbered.

Forty proposed rooms sit at the next free number in their own department. They cover loss of a host, a payment rail, or papers; death or silence of the first drafter; a bought auditor, a hidden voting pact, and a deed that tries to sell the kernel; wash trades and padded counts; a well that is alone, late, or split across two skies; fork, merger, and a self-heal loop that walks a sentence by inches; proposals that queue when no human is left to etch; and the rule that a form, an offset, or a hidden load does not outrank sex, a signed kilowatt-hour, or a locked axiom.

Meters outrank speech. v1.1 does not add a speech exception, a weight, or a path around the terminal gate. It tells a camp to answer lawful process and not to forge a record.

The full threat list, the before-and-after text, and the byte-compare are in [`v1.1-pentest-report.md`](v1.1-pentest-report.md).

## What belongs on the public website

- The version line and the one-paragraph description already at the top of `sapiens-novus.md`: a constitution for decentralized settlements, binary biological reality, a signed energy ledger, meters over speech, built to heal, fork, and scale.
- The map of departments (Praefatio through Omega), unchanged in structure.
- A short note that v1.1 adds continuity, audit, and mesh rules without moving an axiom. Link the rooms, do not paraphrase them into a looser slogan.
- The license (GPL-3.0) and the pointer to the repository.
- Do not put a personal name, a threat-scenario narrative, or this review on the public page. The instrument is the page. The review is for the owner.

## What does not belong on the website

- The open questions from the review.
- Any claim that a later, unreleased edit is already in force.
- Instructions, implied or explicit, for avoiding a court, forging papers, or harming a person. The text does not contain them, and a website should not add them.
