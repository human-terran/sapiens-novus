**Glossary choice.** B.1 Usus was not merged into the Glossary. The Glossary table is the lock of terms. Moving B.1's sentences into that table would have moved numbers and duties from one room to another. The Glossary's Pactum, Codex, and Lex were rewritten around that table. B.1 remains the usage record for terms the cells do not spell, and for the name collisions (two rooms called Horologium, the cite `1.6`, the symbol aliases between I.IV and IX.1, and the verb `uncompromise`).

A room marked VERBATIM was left byte-identical on purpose. The reason is in that room's line. Most of those rooms are capital, custody, clock, or anti-evasion rules whose sentences were already exact, or proposed rooms already written in the target voice. A further paraphrase there was judged more likely to drop a condition than to clarify one.

Where a room was restructured, the chapter section below includes the before text and the after text.
